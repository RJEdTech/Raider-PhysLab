Faith and Physics

Jesuits have done science for almost 500 years. These are true stories where the physics you are learning meets the Catholic, Jesuit tradition, in the order Physics teaches the units. Each one gives the facts with their sources, then you do the math and check it.

Each story also tells what it leaves out, including the hard parts of the history, and ends with a question for reflection. The reflection is yours: there is no right answer to check.

    Questions to ask any number

    • Who counted, and how?
    • Who is missing?
    • Average or median?
    • Per what?
    • Compared to what?
    • What would change the answer?
    • What does this number hide?
